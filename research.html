Research threads

01

Hardware security of analog and in-memory computing

IIT Gandhinagar · 2025–26 · with U. Singh

Analog in-memory computing stores neural-network weights as device conductances and computes directly in the array. That makes it efficient, and it also makes the supply current depend on the stored data. I study how much of that information leaks through the power rail and how easily an adversary can recover it.

Result
On an RRAM 1T1R crossbar in the open SKY130 PDK, power traces alone reveal Hamming-weight leakage (ρ = 0.9979) and allow full weight extraction (ρ = 0.9998).
Recognition
IEEE SSCS Code-a-Chip travel grant (VLSI Symposium 2026); presented at IEEE ESSERC 2026
Methods
SKY130, ngspice, Python-based trace analysis
Publications
ESSERC 2026
02

ESD protection for implantable neurostimulators

M.Tech thesis, IIT Gandhinagar · 2024–25 · advisor Prof. Sandip Lashkare

Neurostimulators for brain–machine interfaces drive high-voltage electrodes from scaled CMOS, and their pads are exposed to discharge events during handling and implantation. My thesis developed ESD clamps that protect 10 V outputs in 65 nm and 180 nm CMOS while keeping the clamping voltage low.

Result
Sub-3 Ω dynamic resistance; IEC 61000-4-2 compliance for 10 V neurostimulator operation
Methods
Transmission-line-pulse (TLP) characterisation, TCAD, Cadence Virtuoso layout
Publications
IEEE JEDS 2025; ICEE 2025; ICEE 2025; EDTM 2025; VDAT 2024
03

RRAM crossbars and in-memory computing

IIT Gandhinagar · 2024–25

I built hardware for RRAM in-memory computing that can be measured on the bench: three crossbar boards (4×4, 8×2 and 8×8) with transimpedance front-ends and ADC read-out, feeding an FPGA classifier that runs a bit-sliced MAC without DSP blocks. Alongside, I designed a hybrid non-volatile SRAM/ReRAM cell and a 12T interlocked SRAM for stable in-memory access.

Result
End-to-end MNIST classification on the hybrid platform; Best Paper Award, IEEE ICEE 2025
Methods
KiCad, Verilog on Basys-3, Verilog-A device models, Spectre
Publications
ICEE 2025
04

Specification-grounded verification tooling

AMD, Hyderabad · 2025–26

Commercial HDL simulators do not always agree on what the IEEE language reference manuals require. I worked on cross-simulator compliance for VHDL-2008/2019 and SystemVerilog, and built retrieval-augmented tools that generate test cases citing the governing clause and run them on XSim, Xcelium, Questa and Riviera-PRO.

Result
A change-request verification flow that went from about three hours to about ten minutes

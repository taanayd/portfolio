01 · Profile

Silicon, Software & Curiosity

I am a Silicon Design Engineer at AMD, Hyderabad, working on cross-simulator RTL verification, LRM compliance testing across VHDL-2019, VHDL-2008, and SystemVerilog against IEEE 1076 and 1800 language standards. Beyond verification, I've built and deployed two AI-powered internal platforms using Claude API and ChromaDB RAG — cutting batch verification from 3 hours to 10 minutes.

My M.Tech at IIT Gandhinagar (CPI 9.06) under Prof. Sandip Lashkare focused on ESD Protection for Brain-Machine Interface SoCs — achieving sub-3Ω dynamic resistance for neurostimulator-grade protection in 65 nm & 180 nm CMOS. The research spans 7 IEEE publications across JEDS, EDTM, ICEE, and VDAT.

"Where circuits meet curiosity — I design silicon and explore the world in equal measure."

When I step away from the lab, I travel — camera in hand, itinerary loose. Photography is my second language.

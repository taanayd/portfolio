Doctoral researcher · COSIC, KU Leuven

Tanay Das

I am a PhD researcher in the COSIC group at KU Leuven, in the Embedded Systems & Security team of Prof. Nele Mentens and Prof. Kris Myny. My doctoral work is on flexible chip design in IGZO thin-film technology, with hardware security at its core.

Before Leuven I was a Silicon Design Engineer at AMD in Hyderabad. I hold an M.Tech in Microelectronics & VLSI from IIT Gandhinagar, where my thesis addressed ESD protection for brain–machine-interface neurostimulators, and a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering from Sikkim Manipal Institute of Technology, graduating with the gold medal.

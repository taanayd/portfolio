01 · About

Building at the edge of
silicon and software

I am a Silicon Design Engineer at AMD, Hyderabad — working on cross-simulator RTL verification and LRM compliance across VHDL-2019, VHDL-2008, and SystemVerilog. Beyond verification, I build AI-powered internal platforms that cut team workflows from hours to minutes.

My M.Tech at IIT Gandhinagar (CPI 9.06) under Prof. Sandip Lashkare focused on ESD Protection for Brain-Machine Interface SoCs — achieving sub-3Ω dynamic resistance in 65nm & 180nm CMOS. The work spans 9 IEEE publications.

When I'm not in the lab or IDE, I'm out exploring Northeast India with a camera — finding the same precision in a landscape that I bring to an analog design problem.

"Where circuits meet curiosity — I design silicon and explore the world in equal measure."

★ SSCS Code-a-Chip Award ESSERC 2026 · Accepted
Power Side-Channel Vulnerability on Analog In-Memory Computing Arrays
T. Das, U. SinghESSERC 2026 · Palma de Mallorca

Demonstrates power side-channel vulnerability on analog IMC arrays using RRAM 1T1R crossbar on SKY130 PDK. Achieves Pearson ρ = 0.9979 for Hamming weight leakage and ρ = 0.9998 for weight extraction — awarded IEEE SSCS Code-a-Chip Travel Grant.

ESD Protection with Sub-3Ω Dynamic Resistance for Neuromodulation SoC
T. Das, N. Ahmad, L. Somappa, S. LashkareIEEE JEDS 2025

Novel ESD protection topology achieving sub-3Ω dynamic resistance for neuromodulation SoC applications in 65nm CMOS. Validated through TLP characterization, achieving IEC 61000-4-2 compliance for 10V neurostimulator operation.

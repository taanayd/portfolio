✦ 2025 IEEE SSCS Code-a-Chip Award ESSERC 2026 · Travel Grant
Power Side-Channel Vulnerability Analysis on Analog In-Memory Computing Arrays
T. Das, U. Singh  ·  Co-authored
IEEE SSCS Code-a-Chip Design Challenge · ESSERC 2026, Palma de Mallorca, Sep 7–10
Demonstrates power side-channel vulnerability on analog in-memory computing (IMC) arrays using RRAM 1T1R crossbar on SKY130 PDK with Ngspice. Achieves Pearson ρ = 0.9979 for Hamming weight leakage and ρ = 0.9998 for weight extraction from power traces. Awarded IEEE SSCS Code-a-Chip Travel Grant for presentation at ESSERC 2026, Palma de Mallorca.

Power Side-Channel Vulnerability on Analog In-Memory Computing Arrays

T. Das, U. Singh

IEEE European Solid-State Electronics Research Conference (ESSERC), Palma de Mallorca, 2026

ConferenceIEEE SSCS Code-a-Chip travel grant (VLSI 2026) Code notebook ↗
Abstract

Shows that analog in-memory computing arrays leak their stored weights through the supply rail. On an RRAM 1T1R crossbar in the open SKY130 PDK, power traces alone reveal Hamming-weight leakage (ρ = 0.9979) and allow full weight extraction (ρ = 0.9998).

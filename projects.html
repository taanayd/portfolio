Research hardware · IIT Gandhinagar

RRAM crossbar platform for in-memory computing

Three custom RRAM crossbar boards with transimpedance front-ends and a multi-channel ADC, connected to an FPGA classifier. The FPGA runs a bit-sliced multiply–accumulate without any DSP blocks and classifies MNIST digits end to end. This co-design work received the Best Paper Award at IEEE ICEE 2025.

Circuit design · IIT Gandhinagar · 2023

Non-volatile SRAM / ReRAM cell

A hybrid memory cell that keeps its state in ReRAM through power-down and serves reads at SRAM speed, together with a 12T interlocked SRAM variant that stays stable under in-memory-computing access patterns. ReRAM behaviour was modelled in Verilog-A and the cells were simulated in Spectre. Projects with Prof. S. Lashkare (NV-SRAM) and Prof. J. Mekie (12T SRAM).

EDA tooling · AMD

LRM-grounded RTL test-case generator

The IEEE 1076 and 1800 language reference manuals are chunked and indexed for retrieval. An engineer chooses a language construct, the relevant clauses are retrieved, and a language model writes test cases that cite them. The suite then runs on four simulators in parallel and divergences are flagged automatically.

EDA tooling · AMD

Change-request batch verification portal

A web interface that pulls assigned change requests from Jira, reads each reproduction procedure, generates the simulation scripts and submits them to the LSF cluster in parallel, with live status streaming. A batch of 18 requests went from about three hours to about ten minutes.
